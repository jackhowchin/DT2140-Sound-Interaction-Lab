    • Interaction 2, Creaking door: the phone is spun while lying on a flat surface.

    We pictured the phone on a table as a door on its hinge. Spinning it is like swinging the door, so it creaks. Spin faster and the creak gets stronger, like more force on the hinge. When the phone stops, the creak stops. We picked the creaking door because that sound comes from friction, and friction sounds tell you how hard someone is pushing.
