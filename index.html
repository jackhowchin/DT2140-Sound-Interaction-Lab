    • Interaction 1, Wind: the phone is placed flat in the hand and tilted from side to side.

    We pictured the phone as a sail or a kite catching a breeze. Tilting it sways it side to side, a bit like something being pushed around by the wind. The more you tilt, the stronger the gust, so the wind gets louder as the angle grows and dies down as you level the phone out. Tilting is a smooth movement, and wind rises and falls smoothly too, so it felt like a natural fit.
